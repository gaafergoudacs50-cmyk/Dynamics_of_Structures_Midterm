Question (2):[8 Points]

A helicopter landing gear consists of a metal framework rather than the coil spring based suspension system used in a fixed-wing aircraft. A prototype of this framework is formed from composite material with unknown modulus E and cross sectional area A. An experiment is performed consisting of forming it into a cantilevered beam of length 1 m and I = 10⁻⁹ m⁴ with a 6 kg mass attached at its end. (Neglect the damping effect.)

a) If the system is given an initial displacement in the lateral direction and found to oscillate with a natural period of 0.5 s. Calculate the modulus of elasticity E in MPa. [4 points]

ωₙ = 2π/τ = 2π/0.5 = 12.57 rad/s
k = mωₙ² = 6 × (12.57)² = 947.5 N/m
Lateral: k = 3EI/l³ → E = k l³/(3I) = 947.5 × 1³/(3 × 10⁻⁹) = 3.16×10¹¹ N/m²
Answer: E = 3.16×10¹¹ Pa = 3.16×10⁵ MPa

b) Then, if the system is given an initial displacement in the axial direction and found to oscillate with a natural frequency of 200 rad/sec. Calculate the cross sectional area that should be used in mm². [4 points]

k = mωₙ² = 6 × 200² = 2.4×10⁵ N/m
Axial: k = EA/l → A = k l/E = 2.4×10⁵ × 1/(3.16×10¹¹) = 7.6×10⁻⁷ m²
Answer: A = 7.6×10⁻⁷ m² = 0.76 mm²
